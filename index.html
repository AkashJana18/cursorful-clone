Will my videos be uploaded to Cursorful's servers?

No, unless you explicitly opt in to cloud features, videos never leave your machine. Videos are rendered locally inside your browser and are saved directly to your computer.

Do you support Firefox?

Firefox is not supported because of missing features in its browser and extension architecture.

What triggers the automatic zoom?

Auto zoom is triggered when two or more clicks happen within a 3 second window. The follow-cursor zoom is maintained as long as clicks keep happening. Two clicks are required instead of one to avoid constant zooming in and out, which is overwhelming and can make people with motion sickness nauseous. You can add, remove and adjust zooms in the editor once the recording is complete.

Does Cursorful record the entire desktop or just the browser?

The desktop apps record the entire desktop, and the editor automatically crops to the active window. The Chrome extension records the screen and crops to your browser window. When a non-browser app is in focus, follow-cursor and automatic zooms stop, because extensions cannot listen to mouse events outside the browser viewport. Fixed zooms can still be added in the editor.

Do you offer a student discount?

The Pro license is a lifetime deal that already includes a limited time discount. Cloud features carry ongoing costs, so additional discounts are not available.
